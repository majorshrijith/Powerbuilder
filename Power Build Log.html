Training rules
  • Warm up for 5–10 minutes and do lighter practice sets before the first major exercise.
  • Use controlled technique and stop each set with about 2–3 good reps left. Do not train to failure.
  • Rest 2–3 minutes between bigger compound exercises and 1–2 minutes between isolation exercises.
  • Increase weight gradually, only when you can complete the top of the rep range with clean form.
  • Learn unfamiliar free-weight movements with a qualified trainer. Avoid maximal lifts.
  • Eat regular meals, hydrate and sleep enough. Reduce volume if you are unusually fatigued.
